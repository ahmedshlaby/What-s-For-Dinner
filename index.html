Chicken Tikka Masala

Rich and creamy Indian curry with tender chicken pieces

Extended Preparation Time

This recipe requires more than 45 minutes to prepare. Plan accordingly!

  1. 600g chicken breast, cubed
  2. 1 cup plain yogurt
  3. 2 tablespoons tikka masala paste
  4. 400ml coconut cream
  5. 1 onion, diced
  6. 24 cloves garlic, minced
  7. 2 tablespoons ginger, grated
  1. Marinate chicken in half the yogurt and 1 tablespoon tikka paste for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan, cook marinated chicken until browned. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, sauté onion until soft. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add remaining tikka paste and canned tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Stir in coconut cream and remaining yogurt. Add chicken back to the pan.
  6. Simmer for 15 minutes until sauce thickens. Garnish with cilantro and serve with rice.
